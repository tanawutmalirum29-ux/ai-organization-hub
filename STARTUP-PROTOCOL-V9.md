# V9 — Temporary advisory council → two deputy-CEO signatures → flexible delivery team

**Binding for NEW `protocol_version: "V9"` rounds only.** Retired V6/V7/V8 rounds stay untouched. The canonical `appointment-CEO/main` remains an organization *template*; a new CEO creates its own `round/<round-id>` GitHub branch and uses its own messages/receipts/chats. This startup protocol supersedes older instructions to hire DIR/TL/Engineers immediately or to let a game advisor send proposals directly to CEO before OWNER acknowledges the presentation.

## Startup — the mandatory sequence

1. **Fresh CEO only.** OWNER gives ONE full appointment. CEO reads all main rules, validates new unique Round/appointment/branch and archives that first Owner chat. CEO initially appoints **exactly two planning roles** via separate one-time full initial appointments: `GAME-ADVISOR` and `GAME-ADVISOR-DEPUTY`. CEO does **not** hire any DCEO, DIR, TL, Engineer or other production role now, and does not select a quest, start implementation, or write game code. Advisor's direct manager is CEO; deputy advisor reports to the advisor for planning but may submit its final independent proposal **directly to CEO**. Both are appointed by CEO during bootstrap, and use fresh chats and full first appointment. The optional independently appointed `ORG-ADVISOR` from earlier governance is *not* part of this game startup and must not be auto-created.
2. **Wait for OWNER — NP or idea.** Advisor does nothing proactive beyond onboarding/reading the game, receiving messages and brief `C`. OWNER may send **`NP`** to GAME-ADVISOR to ask for proposals, or write a free-form game-development idea. Advisor reads game evidence, creates a numbered `ADVISOR_PRESENTATION` in GitHub with the full proposal and provenance (`NP` or Owner idea) and **presents its actual complete suggested work text to OWNER in the advisor chat**. Advisor may exchange explanation in full prose with OWNER in this phase; *all other roles retain short-code-only communication*. Journal the Owner request, visible advisor proposal and response faithfully with public-repo privacy redaction.
3. **Wait for OWNER `C`.** After showing proposal, advisor MUST stop, wait for explicit OWNER `C` **in that adviser chat** and journal that OWNER `C`. Here `C` means "I have seen the full suggestion; proceed to the deputy's independent review", **NOT** a game-release/feature approval or a requirement that Owner select the idea. If OWNER requests change or offers another idea instead of `C`, update proposal and repeat presentation. **No handoff to deputy until an actual journaled `C` follows the presentation.**
4. **Deputy advisor review.** Advisor creates new numbered `ADVISOR_HANDOFF` to `GAME-ADVISOR-DEPUTY`, referencing exact presentation M ID and OWNER `C` journal event. Tell OWNER only `MN-GAME-ADVISOR-DEPUTY`. Deputy reads/receipts all pending messages, reads the game and develops **additional independent alternatives, constraints, balance and tests** (not just copy the main advisor). Deputy saves a numbered structured `PROPOSAL` **directly to CEO**, with original advisor handoff and its own added analysis. Tell OWNER `MN-CEO`. Main advisor may NOT bypass this step with its own proposal to CEO.
5. **Two temporary deputy CEOs.** Only after CEO receives/deems valid the deputy's `PROPOSAL` and its read receipt, CEO appoints exactly **`DCEO-A` and `DCEO-B`** in two fresh chats, one-time complete appointment each. They are **independent** deputy CEOs with the same reporting authority to CEO, no production team appointments or code rights during this stage. Each reads the original/deputy proposals and actual game state, independently critiques business/game design, technical and QA feasibility, risk and staffing.
6. **Two-way debate and signoff.** DCEO-A and DCEO-B exchange numbered `PLAN_REVIEW` messages **to one another via GitHub and OWNER's short `MN` relay**, discussing alternatives until disagreements are resolved, never assume invisible chat communication. An agreed, versioned final plan lives in `workspace/plans/<PLAN_ID>/r<REVISION>.json`, containing scope, non-goals, balancing, acceptance/QA, risk and **custom staffing proposal**. Both separately save numbered `PLAN_SIGNOFF` **to CEO** (actual appointment identity, `APPROVED`, identical plan path+exact git-blob SHA). Before signing, both deputies must have recorded individual read receipts for peer PLAN_REVIEW messages. CEO must read/receipt **both** final PLAN_SIGNOFF messages before PLAN_APPROVAL. Two different messages from two distinct appointees on the **same immutable final plan revision** are necessary; one signature, mismatched SHA/revision, unaddressed objections or self-signing is a blocker. A later plan revision invalidates old signoffs. GitHub identity checks attest recorded role IDs, **not cryptographic person signatures**.
7. **CEO adjudication, retire planners.** CEO checks both signoffs, actual agreed plan and reviewer findings. CEO may reject/return for revision and must not staff prematurely. On accepting, record a numbered `PLAN_APPROVAL` citing exact two signed M IDs and SHA. CEO then saves **four separate `ROLE_RETIRED`** records to OWNER documenting retirement of `GAME-ADVISOR`, `GAME-ADVISOR-DEPUTY`, `DCEO-A` and `DCEO-B`, changes all four roster statuses to `CLOSED`, and archives their chat/receipt history. **Do not send these retired chats any new `MN`/tasks**; they were temporary and are now permanently outside this round's execution chain. Their roster entries remain as CLOSED audit identities.
8. **Only then: form the build team.** CEO chooses the **smallest, shallowest reasonable execution structure** for the signed plan, respecting proposal rationale and QA separation: `CEO → Engineer` (small job), `CEO → DIR → Engineer` or `CEO → TL → Engineer` (medium), or `CEO → DIR → TL → Engineers` only when workload truly requires it. Multiple DIRs/teams allowed; **do not enforce a fixed number of people or departments**. A DIR can supervise directly or delegate to max 2 TL, and a TL max 3 Engineers; CEO can directly supervise an E for a simple one-person job. If no coding needed, choose no Engineers and document why. Each actual team role must have a fresh one-time detailed appointment, direct accountable manager, exclusive file scope and independent reviewer/QA owner. CEO issues `MISSION_CHARTER` bound to the **signed plan SHA** to the chosen delivery lead. No game code before this gate.
9. **Release/close as before.** Preserve independent QA and actual CI, CEO `RELEASE` before merging game main, then verified SHA, issues and final round auto-close. OWNER carries codes, not merge votes.

## Allowed stages in `workspace/round.json.startup_phase`

`CEO_SETUP` → `WAIT_NP` → `WAIT_OWNER_C` → `DEPUTY_REVIEW` → `DCEO_CONSENSUS` → `EXECUTION` → final CLOSED round. Phase changes are durable only after corresponding append-only messages, Owner journal C, signatures and role status evidence exist. **New V9 rounds must use `protocol_version: "V9"`.** Stages track durable *facts*, not permission to skip steps.

## Canonical record shapes

- `ADVISOR_PRESENTATION` (GAME-ADVISOR→OWNER): `presentation: {source: "NP" | "OWNER_IDEA", proposal_text: string, observed_game_evidence: string}`. Full proposal text is deliberately shown in advisor chat, archived in `workspace/chats/<ADVISOR_APPOINTMENT>/events/`.
- `ADVISOR_HANDOFF` (GAME-ADVISOR→GAME-ADVISOR-DEPUTY): `handoff: {presentation_message_id: string, owner_c_journal_path: "workspace/chats/<advisor>/events/0000NN.json"}`, with the original `OWNER: C` event written **after** the advisor's `AI: proposal` event.
- `PROPOSAL` (GAME-ADVISOR-DEPUTY→CEO): existing six mandatory `proposal` fields, plus `advisor_handoff_message_id` and `independent_findings` describing the added work. CEO must process this message before DCEO appointments.
- `PLAN_REVIEW` DCEO-A↔DCEO-B: `review: {assessment: string, verdict: "DISCUSS" | "AGREE" | "NEEDS_REVISION", references: string}`, always archived to other DCEO's M inbox.
- `PLAN_SIGNOFF` DCEO-X→CEO: `signoff: {plan_path: string, plan_sha: string, decision: "APPROVED", signature_role: "DCEO-X", signature_appointment_id: string, rationale: string}`. Plan content includes `plan_id`, `revision`, `scope`, `non_goals`, `acceptance_criteria`, `qa_plan`, `balance_analysis`, `risk_analysis`, `staffing_proposal`, `consensus_resolution`; Git blob SHA is computed from exact bytes (not a fabricated hash).
- `PLAN_APPROVAL` CEO→OWNER: `approval: {signoff_message_ids: [two IDs], plan_path: string, plan_sha: string, rationale: string}`.
- `ROLE_RETIRED` CEO→OWNER: `retirement: {role_id: string, appointment_id: string, approved_plan_sha: string, reason: string}`. After all four, their roster status must be CLOSED; no more communications with those retired ChatGPT conversations.
- `MISSION_CHARTER` CEO→chosen lead: normal scoped mission fields plus `mission.approved_plan_sha` exactly matching CEO approval; original V8 QA requirements still apply.

Owner-visible codes: `NP` (ask GAME-ADVISOR for proposal); advisor outputs **full** content once and waits for Owner `C`; after that `MN-GAME-ADVISOR-DEPUTY`, `MN-CEO`, `MN-DCEO-A`, `MN-DCEO-B`, and eventually `AP-ROLE` for execution staffing. `NX` checks this phase plus actual inbox/receipts and never asks OWNER to decide scope.

## Safety and compatibility

- Only the GAME-ADVISOR presenting a new idea and the existing ORG-ADVISOR discussing governance may use meaningful full prose with OWNER. The optional ORG-ADVISOR is **not** auto-appointed by new CEO. Full content visible to OWNER still needs a GitHub record; sensitive details are redacted from **public** repos or kept on approved private storage.
- `C` at the proposal step is a verified **presentation acknowledgement**, not the "complete all work" meaning used elsewhere: context decides; it unlocks deputy review only.
- No cross-chat auto-sending, no imaginary signatures, no editing old numbered messages. Owner transports short `MN` codes for each next chat. The signature gate is **AI-level**; final release stays CEO-authorized with independent QA and real CI.
