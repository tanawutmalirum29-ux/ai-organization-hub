## 1. The hard-cap counting rule (GLOBAL_MAX_AI = 10)
- Count each distinct **ChatGPT AI conversation/person operating as an appointed organization role** once **across the entire organization**, whether CEO, Deputy, Director, Lead, Engineer, Assistant, Roster Steward, reviewer or a cross-team helper, and whether currently busy or awaiting a new task. Multiple teams or extra roles do NOT reset the count.
- **Staffing status must reflect reality.** The active staff set is defined by verified real appointments/ACKs not formally retired. `ACTIVE`, `STANDBY`, `ACK_VERIFIED`, and `OPERATIONALLY_ACTIVE` occupy the global headcount until an authorized `ROLE_RETIRED` / handoff/closure is recorded. `PENDING_ACK` with an actual recipient's ACK also occupies a **provisional seat** pending reconciliation; it cannot be ignored because it has not reached JSON yet. Unassigned templates and genuinely unaccepted capacity slots are not staff. Archived, genuinely relieved roles do not consume an operating seat, but the archived conversation remains historical proof, not a newly activated person.
- Any attempted new appointment must first reconcile all verified ACK evidence, including separate appointment Issues not yet merged into `TEAM-REGISTRY.json`. If the count is `>=10`, **do not issue another appointment, instantiate another AI chat or authorize a new staffing seat**. No simultaneous direct manager appointment, Assistant creation, cross-team hidden contributor, or temporary "11th" can evade this.
- To replace one AI: the authorized CEO/Delivery first defines the successor's responsibility and performs a **logged `ROLE_RETIRED / RESPONSIBILITY_TRANSFER`** for a vacated seat, with ownership, open PRs, blockers, next reviewer and exact Issue/ACK references. Then a replacement may be appointed within the ten-person global ceiling. Any outstanding Task permissions of a retired role become invalid. Do not fake that an old ACK never happened.
- Only Owner can change the ten-person policy in a later explicit instruction; **CEO cannot raise the limit for a giant task**. Instead reduce concurrent scope, sequence delivery waves, reuse manager expertise, pause lower priorities or combine duties among the existing AI.
