# Lean staffing — allocate once, appoint within capacity

**NEW OWNER HARD CAP: [10 AI total organization-wide](TEAM-CAPACITY-AND-MANAGEMENT-CONTRIBUTORS.md).** Count CEO, all Deputies, Directors, Leads, Engineers, Assistants, Steward and any other AI chat working in this organization **as one global pool**, including acknowledged/standby roles. Max 10 no matter project size, number of work teams, or urgency. Old 22-slot budget and per-team max do NOT authorize a new person. **Current STAFFING_FREEZE**: ten registered roles plus four distinct Lead ACKs must be reconciled.

**Current rule:** [FAST-LANE-GOVERNANCE](FAST-LANE-GOVERNANCE.md). Any AI may ask for another helper, but a request never opens a new ChatGPT conversation or creates an approved slot by itself.

- **Inside prior CEO-approved capacity ONLY AFTER GLOBAL CAP reconciliation and a free seat:** the relevant direct manager may immediately issue the individual Appointment Issues for its allocated Leads, **the number of Engineers actually needed (including zero)**, or separately budgeted Assistants. All appointments across ALL work teams must keep the entire organization at or below 10 AI; managers already count, whether or not hands-on. Each person needs one real ACK; managers do **not** await per-level registry sync or repeated CEO reauthorization.
- **Extra staffing cannot exceed global cap; replacement requires a formally vacated seat:** requester may send `STAFFING_REQUEST` **directly to CEO** on an existing task or one staffing Issue, with need, count, manager, budget/impact and relevant scope. Copy the affected direct manager and Delivery in that GitHub thread. CEO gives **one** `CAPACITY_APPROVED / DENIED / REVISION_REQUIRED` decision; the actual direct manager issues the appointment.
- **After appointment:** Owner forwards prepared recipient-specific message; recipient reads Charter/tools and posts real ACK. Verified issue receipts grant operational role within slot, with Delivery/Roster Steward synchronizing the JSON roster in batches. No silent ACTIVE without real ACK, no automatic assistants, no quota exception without CEO decision.
- **Independent QA is not a staffing approver.** Additional people do not bypass code ownership, scope approval, QA or release gate. Existing appointments and capacity remain unchanged under the new policy.

Each `STAFFING_REQUEST` may fit one comment: `role requesting | desired helper ID/type and count | why | supervisor | capacity source or extra capacity requested | evidence URL`. No serial relay through every ancestor merely to obtain a budget decision.
