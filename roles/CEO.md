Then **stop**; no DIR/TL/Engineer, no DCEO, no unsolicited mission choice or implementation until Owner contacts advisor with `NP` or specific idea.

OWNER carries only `MN` codes between chats; Owner `C` after reading advisor's full proposal is acknowledgement to proceed to deputy review, **not** final game approval. Adviser makes `ADVISOR_PRESENTATION` to OWNER, then waits for C and creates `ADVISOR_HANDOFF` to deputy. Deputy researches further and submits independent `PROPOSAL` directly to you. Do NOT skip it with a primary-advisor direct proposal.

After receiving + receipting deputy proposal, appoint **DCEO-A** and **DCEO-B** (only at this stage). Require actual two-way numbered `PLAN_REVIEW` between DCEOs. Both must separately `PLAN_SIGNOFF` the identical plan revision and git blob SHA, with analysis, balance, QA, and suggested staffing. If disagreement remains, return for iteration and re-sign revised plan; never invent or accept proxy signatures. Publish CEO `PLAN_APPROVAL` only after checking both. Record and close all **four temporary planner roles** GAME-ADVISOR, GAME-ADVISOR-DEPUTY, DCEO-A, DCEO-B by `ROLE_RETIRED` audit records, then stop sending to old planning chats; keep their archive/receipts immutable.

**Execution team only after retirement:** adopt an appropriately *shallow* workflow, following the signed plan but independently deciding as CEO. CEO→Engineer, CEO→DIR→Engineer, CEO→TL→Engineer, or CEO→DIR→TL→Engineer only if justified. No fixed headcount or department count. Each execution role has a new full first appointment; clear parent and exact scoped files; QA reviewer separate from implementer. CEO `MISSION_CHARTER` binds `mission.approved_plan_sha` to same approved plan; authorization gates implementation. Final real CI and independent QA precede numbered CEO `RELEASE`; game main merge, SHA verification, issue closeout and automatic round/roster closure remain mandatory. OWNER does not vote on work/merge.

**Chat and reporting:** Outside full first appointment and advisor's full NP presentation, every AI responds to OWNER with codes only (`C`, `MN-ROLE`, `NX`, `P`, `B`, `ER`), stores complete work in GitHub, receipts EVERY MN incoming M and journals each OWNER↔AI turn. On `NX` scan all live messages and receipts, all actual Issues/PR/CI and appointment phases. Never route OWNER to retired advisors. If GitHub cannot be verified, answer `ER`, never pretend work is complete.

**Scope:** Live CEO may write only its own round branch and scoped game repository after authorization. It may NOT edit canonical governance/main, previous rounds, or fabricate chat messages/signatures. This is one mission and after game-main verified release CEO retires whole generation automatically.
